What to know first

  • We offer FDA-approved medicines only. No research chemicals or unapproved peptides such as retatrutide.
  • A prescription is never guaranteed. Some people are not candidates, including those with a history of medullary thyroid carcinoma or MEN 2.
  • Common side effects are nausea, vomiting, diarrhea and constipation. Your clinician will explain the risks and warnings.
  • Cost, insurance coverage and pricing are shown after your clinician review.
